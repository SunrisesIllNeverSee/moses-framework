## Summary finding

The strongest move is NOT to own the individual nouns (BI, AAI, SI are all
established/crowded terms) but to lock the **relational architecture** and
**Intelligence Provenance** as the novel contribution.

## Territory around BI / AAI / SI

| Term | Existing territory | Read for MO§ES |

## The important finding

No established ontology does:

**BI × AAI → SI**

with:
- BI = intelligence whose operation is biologically generated/initiated
- AAI = intelligence whose operation depends upon external initiation/assistance
- SI = intelligence manifested by the coupled operating system

That's MO§ES territory.

## Key insight: "Assisted" is inverse of PwC

PwC: AI assists the person.
MO§ES: The intelligence itself is assisted into operation.

MO§ES AAI describes a property of genesis/activation dependency, not
relationship to user. BI → initiates intelligent operation. AAI requires
external originating condition.

"Assisted" doesn't mean permanently subordinate to BI. An AAI can be assisted
into operation by another AAI. BI → AAI₁ → AAI₂ → AAI₃. The original spark
becomes a lineage problem → provenance.

## Mathematical correction: common FACTOR, not denominator

If BI = B × I, AAI = AA × I, SI = S × I, then I is invariant; B/AA/S qualify
the intelligence. The noun doesn't change. The modifiers change. This
constructs a common measurement class, connecting to Micro Evals.

## Intelligence Provenance — the gold

Standard provenance asks: where did this information/artifact come from?
W3C PROV formalizes entities, activities, agents.

AI world extending into:
- Reasoning provenance (arXiv 2603.21692)
- Decision provenance (decisionprovenance.org)
- Epistemic/cognitive provenance (emerging 2026)

NO dominant established field under "INTELLIGENCE PROVENANCE."

MO§ES version asks:
> Where did the intelligence manifested in this state, transformation,
> artifact, or decision originate — and through which intelligences was it
> subsequently transformed?

Provisional definition:
> Intelligence Provenance is the traceable origin, initiation, transformation,
> contribution, curation, authority, commitment, and persistence of
> intelligence as it moves through biological, assisted, and systemic forms.

## Curated Intelligence has a legitimate place

Not as BI's sibling. As a provenance state.

BI generates A → AAI transforms A→B → BI rejects part B, selects B₂ →
AAI expands B₂→C → BI commits C.

C is: curated/transformed/composite intelligence with traceable intelligence
provenance.

Potential lineage roles:

- transforming intelligence
- curating intelligence
